Privacy

ContractorQuote runs entirely in your web browser. There's no account and no server that receives what you type.

What is stored

To save your work, the calculator keeps your current draft quote and your business details in your browser's local storage on this device. Nothing else is stored.

What is sent to us

Nothing you enter. The site doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Your hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) when the site files are downloaded.

How to delete your data

  • Use Start a new quote in the calculator to clear the current draft, or
  • clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove everything, including your business details.

Because your data only lives in this browser, it won't appear on your other devices, and it will be lost if you clear your browser data. Save or print quotes you want to keep.

ContractorQuote is a free estimating aid. Figures are estimates based on what you enter and are not financial, tax or legal advice.

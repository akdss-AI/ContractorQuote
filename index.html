Why "cost plus a bit" loses money

Hidden labour costs

Your hourly rate isn't the whole cost of an hour. Holidays, travel time, insurance and admin all add to it. ContractorQuote adds a labour burden so they don't come out of your profit. How labour burden works

Overhead on every job

The van, phone, tools and accountant have to be paid for by the jobs you do. Add a share of those running costs to each job so they're covered. How to add overhead

Margin, not just markup

Adding 20% to your costs does not give you a 20% profit margin. The calculator works from the margin you want and shows you the markup it takes to get there. Markup vs margin explained

How it works

  1. Enter your costs. Labour hours and rate, labour burden, materials, subcontractors, other job costs and overhead.
  2. Set your profit margin. See the recommended price, your profit, and the equivalent markup straight away.
  3. Add the quote details. Customer, scope of work, exclusions, payment terms and how long the quote is valid.
  4. Print or save as PDF. The quote shows your customer the work and the price, never your costs or profit.

That price leaves £1,026.67 profit, which is 25% of the price. Getting there takes a 33.3% markup on cost. Adding just 25% to the cost would give £3,850.00, which is only a 20% margin.

Common questions

Is it really free?
Yes. There's no account, no subscription and no payment.
Where is my data stored?
Only in your web browser on this device. Nothing you type is sent to us. Read our privacy page.
Does it work out VAT or tax?
No. ContractorQuote doesn't calculate VAT or tax. If you need to mention VAT on a quote, you can add your own wording in the notes field.
Is there a blank quote template?
Yes. Our free builder's quote template can be printed or saved as a PDF and filled in by hand, and it shows a filled-in example.
Can I add my logo?
Not yet. The quote shows your business name and contact details.
